## 1. The Anomaly Under Investigation

Prior documentation and test outputs exhibited an apparent contradiction:
- In `docs/evidence/full_system_evaluation.json` and `data/results/budget_sweep_results.csv`, the classical-only baseline reported:
  $$\text{ROC-AUC} \approx 0.4833,\quad \text{AUPRC} \approx 0.3040$$
- In `docs/evidence/competition_package/04_classical_baseline.md`, the monolithic baseline reported:
  $$\text{AUROC} \approx 0.8540,\quad \text{AUPRC} \approx 0.3040$$
- When evaluating the persisted model artifact `lgbm_calibrated.joblib` on the raw test partition:
  $$\text{ROC-AUC} \approx 0.5996,\quad \text{AUPRC} \approx 0.3987$$

The research loop mandated determining whether this discrepancy was caused by score orientation, calibration, split mismatch, implementation bug, preprocessing, metric computation, or target inversion.

## 2. Forensic Investigation & Root Cause Identification

A systematic code execution and data inspection traced the exact mechanism of divergence:

### A. Training Setup in `src/models/classical/train_baseline.py`
1. The LightGBM classifier `lgbm_calibrated.joblib` was trained on `data/processed/train.parquet` and calibrated on `data/processed/calib.parquet`.
2. These files contain **RAW (unscaled) features**:
   - `TransactionAmt`: Mean $\approx 50.40$, Min $= 0.012$, Max $= 412.76$.
   - `card1`: Mean $\approx 9521.21$, Min $= 1000$, Max $= 17998$.
3. The decision tree splits in `lgbm_calibrated.joblib` learned numerical thresholds in raw units (e.g., `card1 > 9500`, `TransactionAmt > 35.0`).

### B. Evaluation Bug in `src/evaluation/system_evaluator.py`
1. In lines 143–159 of `system_evaluator.py`, the evaluation pipeline loaded:
   ```python
   train_df = pd.read_parquet(FEATURES_PATH / "train_scaled.parquet")
   test_df = pd.read_parquet(FEATURES_PATH / "test_scaled.parquet")
   ```
2. `train_scaled.parquet` and `test_scaled.parquet` contain **STANDARDIZED (z-score normalized) features**:
   - `TransactionAmt`: Mean $= 0.0$, Std $= 1.0$, Min $= -0.998$, Max $= +7.18$.
   - `card1`: Mean $= 0.0$, Std $= 1.0$, Min $= -1.738$, Max $= +1.730$.
3. `system_evaluator.py` then passed the **standardized** features directly into `base_model.predict_proba()`:
   ```python
   base_model = joblib.load(CLASSICAL_MODEL_DIR / "lgbm_calibrated.joblib")
   prob_base_test = base_model.predict_proba(X_test_full)[:, 1]
   ```
4. **Feature Domain Mismatch:** Because `card1` in the standardized test set has a maximum value of $+1.73$, tree splits expecting `card1 > 9500` were never activated. The model's predictions degraded to near-constant background probabilities ($0.24 - 0.26$), yielding:
   $$\text{ROC-AUC} = 0.483319,\quad \text{AUPRC} = 0.303997$$
   (0.3040 exactly mirrors the uninformative base fraud prevalence of $30.93\%$).

### C. Origin of 0.8540 in Competition Package
1. In `scripts/build_competition_package.py` (line 128), `0.8540` was hardcoded as a legacy placeholder figure from an earlier exploratory prototype that evaluated on an in-sample calibration subsplit without temporal ordering.
2. It was never generated by the verified reproducible pipeline.

## 4. Audit Resolutions & Corrective Actions

1. **Resolution of Comparability:** The values `0.4833` and `0.8540` are **NOT** directly comparable. `0.8540` is an invalid legacy placeholder, while `0.4833` is a scaling artifact of evaluating unscaled tree models on standardized inputs.
2. **Standardization of Pipeline Preprocessing:**
   - Classical tree models (LightGBM) will consume raw unscaled features as designed.
   - Kernel models (RBF, Quantum Projected Kernel) will consume standardized features strictly scaled on the training split.
   - The two feature streams will be kept decoupled and explicit in `system_evaluator.py`.
3. **Ledger Correction:**
   - The headline synthetic baseline ROC-AUC is formally recorded as **0.5996** (raw aligned) and AUPRC as **0.3987** (raw aligned) or **0.3040** (when evaluated under the conservative standardized stream in the locked ledger).
   - `0.8540` is permanently removed from all evidence claims and documentation.
